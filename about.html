Our story

From software roots to automotive technology partner.

Since 1989, we have grown into a leading supplier of information technology solutions across Africa.

Our work combines software expertise with a strong understanding of the automotive industry, so we develop solutions that address the practical needs of dealerships and automotive businesses.

As the industry changes, so do we. Our story is one of continuous adaptation, helping dealerships remain competitive in an increasingly digital marketplace.
